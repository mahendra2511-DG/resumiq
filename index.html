FAQ

Questions, answered

Will Resumiq guarantee I pass an ATS?

No tool can. Every company configures its ATS differently. Resumiq follows what reliably works — single-column layouts, standard headings, real text (not images), and keywords backed by evidence — and explains its scores so you know exactly what to change.

Does Dexa make things up?

Dexa is instructed to use only the facts you give it. When a number or detail would help, it writes a [placeholder] for you to fill. Always read AI suggestions before using them; you're responsible for what your resume says.

Is my data private?

Without an account, everything stays in your browser. With an account, your documents are stored in your own private rows (other users can't read them). Text is sent to the AI provider only when you click a Dexa action. You can export or delete everything from Settings.

Can I import my current resume?

Yes: upload a PDF or Word (.docx) file, or paste text. Resumiq splits it into sections for you to review. Scanned image PDFs can't be read; paste the text instead.

Should a fresher's resume be one page?

Yes, for most students and freshers. Lead with skills and projects, keep education clear, and drop personal details like date of birth. Resumiq's level setting adjusts section order and length targets for you.

Do I need a photo on my resume?

For most jobs in India and abroad, no. Photos, date of birth, marital status and the "declaration" take space without helping, and can invite bias. Use the space for results.
