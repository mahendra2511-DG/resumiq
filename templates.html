📄 Templates

ATS-friendly resume templates

Every template is single-column with standard headings and real text, so applicant tracking systems can read it. Pick the one that suits your field; you can switch any time without losing content.

What makes a template ATS-friendly?

  • One column, read top to bottom
  • Standard section names (Experience, Education, Skills)
  • Real text, not images or text inside shapes
  • Dates in one consistent format
  • No tables for layout, no header/footer text that holds key info
  • Readable fonts at 10–12 pt
  • No photos, icons for contact details, or skill-level bars
  • Saved as PDF unless the job asks for Word
