👋 About

Built by a trainer, for job seekers

Resumiq exists because good resume advice shouldn't be expensive, and AI shouldn't make things up.

Resumiq is made by Mahendra Singh, a Data Analyst trainer who has helped many learners prepare their first analytics resumes. It's part of CrackAnalytics, a free learning platform for aspiring Data Analysts.

What we believe

  • Truth first. A resume is a promise. Dexa, our AI coach, works only from your real facts and marks missing numbers as placeholders.
  • Role-specific beats generic. A Data Analyst, a nurse and a sales manager need different resumes. Resumiq adapts to your role and level.
  • Explain the score. Every score shows its checks and weights, so you know what to fix.
  • Free and private by default. Use it without an account; your data stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in.
